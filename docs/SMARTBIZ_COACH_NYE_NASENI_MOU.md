## 1. RECITALS & BACKGROUND

**WHEREAS:**

A. **SMARTBIZ COACH** has engineered and operationalized a proprietary, mobile-first, offline-resilient Artificial Intelligence Business Operating System and Unified Commerce Engine (hosted at `smartbizcoach.com.ng` and `market.smartbizcoach.com.ng`). The platform integrates 5-second point-of-sale (POS) retail bookkeeping, offline Progressive Web App (PWA) cashbooks, phone camera barcode scanning, customer debt ledger recovery ("Gbege Book"), bankable AI business plan modeling, generative marketing studios, alternative machine learning credit scoring (SmartScore™ 300–850), and nationwide inter-state B2B wholesale trade infrastructure specifically calibrated for Nigerian market dynamics.

B. **NACCIMA YOUTH ENTREPRENEURS (NYE)** is the statutory organized private sector youth leadership body chartered to organize, accelerate, formalize, and represent youth-owned micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs), commercial traders, industrial manufacturers, and service providers across the 36 States of the Federation and the Federal Capital Territory (FCT), operating under the auspices of NACCIMA Senior Chambers.

C. **ALIGNMENT WITH NASENI INNOVATORS & TECHNICAL BENEFICIARIES:** Recognizing that industrial self-reliance, engineering fabrication, clean-tech, hardware manufacturing, and technical vocational entrepreneurship represent the cornerstone of Nigeria’s economic revitalization, both Parties mutually agree to expand this strategic partnership to deliberately encompass, integrate, and empower innovators, incubatees, and technical beneficiaries of the **National Agency for Science and Engineering Infrastructure (NASENI)**, alongside NYE's commercial trading network.

D. Following extensive technical validation, bilateral consultative meetings, and pilot deployments in October 2026, both Parties have established an institutional framework to deploy SmartBiz Coach nationwide to achieve enterprise formalization, cashflow transparency, inter-state B2B trade, and access to capital for youth traders, technical innovators, and engineering startups.

## 2. PURPOSE AND STRATEGIC OBJECTIVES

The purpose of this MOU is to define the operational, technical, and commercial terms under five (5) strategic pillars:

1. **Digitalization & Formalization of Youth MSMEs:** Transitioning informal, unbanked, and cash-based youth enterprises into auditable, credit-ready, bankable businesses through localized offline POS bookkeeping, petty cash tracking, and financial discipline software.
2. **Industrial & Technical Commercialization for NASENI Beneficiaries:** Catalyzing direct market linkages for engineering prototypes, indigenous hardware, renewable energy assemblies, agro-allied machinery, and fabricated components through dedicated verification, MOQ structuring, and nationwide waybill logistics.
3. **National B2B Commerce & Inter-State Trade Integration:** Integrating NYE and NASENI wholesalers, manufacturers, and verified artisans into a centralized National Market Square (`market.smartbizcoach.com.ng`) featuring verified merchant badges, secure escrow-style order routing, and direct Paystack settlement.
4. **Alternative Credit Scoring & Capital Mobilization:** Deploying algorithmic cashflow verification (SmartScore™ 300–850) and cryptographic audit dossiers to de-risk micro-lending, eliminate collateral barriers, and connect members directly to Bank of Industry (BOI), SMEDAN, and commercial credit programs.
5. **Nationwide Capacity Building & Digital Bootcamps:** Delivering hands-on zonal digital transformation masterclasses, financial literacy workshops, and technical onboarding clinics across all geopolitical zones of Nigeria.

## 3. SCOPE OF COLLABORATION & 24-MODULE PLATFORM ARCHITECTURE

The deployment under this agreement encompasses SmartBiz Coach's comprehensive twenty-four (24) module ecosystem:

### A. Real-Time Financial Operations, POS & Shop Security
1. **Executive Dashboard:** Centralized telemetry command center monitoring live net margin, daily revenue velocity, expense burn, and quick operational shortcuts.
2. **5-Second POS Retail Day-Book & Cashbook:** Rapid checkout recording cash vs. electronic transfers, petty cash, generator fuel/NEPA bills, staff wages, and "Oga Drawings" with automatic daily profit/loss computation.
3. **Phone Camera Barcode & QR Scanner:** Hardware-free inventory checkout utilizing standard mobile phone cameras to scan product barcodes and generate digital sales receipts.
4. **Anti-Theft Apprentice Shift Lock (4-Digit PIN):** Role-based cashier mode allowing shop attendants to record sales while locking sensitive financial ledgers, margins, and admin settings behind an owner PIN.
5. **Open-Market Offline PWA Mode:** Full functional capability in remote open markets with zero internet, automatically synchronizing queued transactions once cellular connectivity resumes.
6. **"Gbege Book" (Digital Debtor Ledger):** Comprehensive customer credit tracking with automated polite WhatsApp and SMS debt recovery nudges to protect working capital against default.
7. **Invoices & Instant Receipts Generator:** Professional mobile billing with PDF export, audit trails, and instant sharing to buyers via WhatsApp.

### B. Machine Learning Financial Intelligence & Credit Readiness
8. **SmartScore™ Alternative Credit Underwriting (300–850):** Algorithmic credit scoring evaluating cashflow velocity, sales consistency, expense ratios, and customer retention without requiring traditional land collateral.
9. **Till Integrity Anomaly Engine (Isolation Forest ML):** AI telemetry detecting transaction irregularities, unrecorded cash leakages, and phantom ledger edits to ensure bank-grade compliance.
10. **Public Cryptographic Audit Dossier (`PublicAuditReport`):** Real-time verifiable digital audit certificates shareable with loan officers at BOI, SMEDAN, DBN, and commercial banks to expedite lending approvals.

### C. Unified National Commerce & Storefront Infrastructure
11. **Central Market Square (`market.smartbizcoach.com.ng`):** High-visibility national multi-vendor directory featuring 4 trade pillars (🛍️ Retail Shop, 💼 Professional Services, 🏢 Real Estate, ⚡ B2B Wholesale).
12. **Standalone WhatsApp Web Storefronts (`smartbizcoach.com.ng/store/{slug}`):** Instant branded e-commerce shopfronts for each member with custom catalog links, product variants, and direct WhatsApp 1-tap ordering.
13. **Direct Paystack Settlement Engine:** Automated split-payment routing settling buyer payments directly into the vendor’s registered Nigerian bank account without platform fund withholding.
14. **State-by-State Delivery Rate Matrix & Order Tracker (`/track/{code}`):** Integrated logistics waybill calculation across all 36 States + FCT with live buyer package tracking.
15. **Verified Customer Reviews & Trust Badges:** 5-star customer review mechanism, verified buyer tags, and anti-fraud seller verification seals.

### D. Generative AI Marketing, Branding & Creative Studios
16. **AI Brand Builder:** Automated generation of corporate brand identities, vision/mission statements, color schemes, and professional logo design suites.
17. **AI Content Studio & Vernacular Copywriter:** Generative marketing engine producing localized social media captions, advert campaigns, and broadcast scripts in English, Pidgin, Hausa, Yoruba, and Igbo.
18. **Snap-to-Studio 4K AI Photo Studio:** Automated background replacement converting casual smartphone photos into studio-grade commercial product imagery.
19. **Marketing Agent & Broadcast HQ:** Multi-channel customer broadcast suite managing targeted SMS (via Termii at ₦6/segment) and automated WhatsApp marketing campaigns.
20. **AI Sales Assistant & Objection Closer:** Conversational sales script engine generating tailored negotiation responses to close deals on WhatsApp.

### E. Capital Linkages, Institutional Compliance & Governance
21. **AI Bankable Business Plan Generator:** 5-year financial models incorporating NGN inflation dynamics, CAPEX/OPEX amortization, and break-even calculations compliant with BOI, SMEDAN, and DBN guidelines.
22. **Grant & Capital Matcher:** Algorithmic matching tool connecting businesses directly with open federal, state, and international grants and intervention funds.
23. **CAC Accredited Agent Legal Desk:** Step-by-step regulatory portal facilitating Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) Business Name and Ltd registrations, Tax Identification Numbers (TIN), and annual statutory filings.
24. **Partner Portal & Executive Control Center (`dashboard/settings`):** Institutional administrative console providing NYE and NASENI leadership with anonymized macro-economic telemetry, member onboarding counts, GMV analytics, and exportable CSV audit records.

## 4. PARTIALLY FREE (HYBRID FREEMIUM) COMMERCIAL FRAMEWORK

To ensure maximum grassroots adoption while maintaining sustainable infrastructure, SmartBiz Coach operates on a **Partially Free (Hybrid Freemium)** model rather than an unsustainable 100% free claim:

### 4.1 100% Free Core Operating Utilities (Lifetime 0 Credits)
Registered NYE members and NASENI beneficiaries enjoy permanent, unrestricted, zero-cost access to all foundational daily utilities:
* **Unlimited Daily Day-Book & Cashbook POS entries** (Cash, Transfer, Credit sales).
* **Phone Camera Barcode & QR Code Inventory Scanner**.
* **Anti-Theft Apprentice Shift Lock (4-Digit Security PIN)**.
* **Offline Open Market PWA functionality with zero data usage**.
* **Petty Cash, Logistics, and Generator Fuel Expense Logging**.
* **Daily WhatsApp Automated Profit/Loss Health Summary Reports**.
* **Gbege Book Customer Debtor Tracking & WhatsApp Debt Reminders**.
* **Public Storefront Listing & WhatsApp Direct Buyer Ordering**.

### 4.2 Micro-Priced Advanced AI & Heavy Compute Services (BizCredit Wallet)
Advanced artificial intelligence, high-compute graphics, telephony, and premium marketplace positioning utilize the platform’s localized Naira wallet (**BizCredits**) at micro-rates designed for Nigerian micro-enterprises:
* **Bank-Ready BOI/SMEDAN 5-Year Business Plan Generation (PDF):** 15 BizCredits (~₦150).
* **Snap-to-Studio 4K AI Product Background Removal:** 2 BizCredits (~₦20 per photo).
* **AI Generative Brand Identity Kit & Logo Generation:** 10 BizCredits (~₦100).
* **Marketplace VIP Promoted Listing Boosts (7-Day Nationwide Spotlight):** 20 BizCredits (~₦200).
* **Automated WhatsApp Follow-Up & Sales Reminders:** 1 BizCredit (~₦10).
* **Promotional SMS Broadcasts (GSM-7 via Termii API):** Billed directly at ₦6 per SMS segment.

### 4.3 Unified BizCredit Wallet Packages
Members can recharge their wallet on demand via Paystack instant transfer or card at transparent, unified rates:
* **Micro Pack:** ₦500 → **50 BizCredits** (~₦10/credit, never expire).
* **Starter Pack:** ₦1,500 → **150 BizCredits** (Includes 7-day Market Square boost + photo AI).
* **Grower Pack:** ₦3,500 → **400 BizCredits** *(+50 Bonus Credits, Verified Merchant Badge)*.
* **Vendor Pro Pack:** ₦7,500 → **1,000 BizCredits** *(+250 Bonus Credits, 30-Day VIP Boost)*.
* **Mogul Pack:** ₦15,000 → **2,500 BizCredits** *(Best Rate: ₦6/credit, heavy business plan modeling)*.

## 5. THE NASENI / NYE BENEFICIARY TECH-STARTUP PACK

To accelerate the commercialization of homegrown Nigerian engineering, technical innovations, and youth manufacturing, the Parties hereby establish the **"SmartBiz Coach × NASENI / NYE Beneficiary Tech-Startup Pack"**:

### 5.1 Qualification Criteria
Beneficiaries shall include:
1. Technical incubatees and innovators affiliated with NASENI Development Institutes and technology hubs.
2. Engineering startups, fabricators, renewable energy installers, and hardware innovators accredited by NASENI or NYE.
3. Youth graduates of technical and vocational education (TVET) establishing commercial hardware or service ventures.

### 5.2 Startup Pack Components & Entitlements
Each verified NASENI / NYE beneficiary onboarded under this partnership receives:
1. **Pre-Funded Onboarding AI BizCredits:** An initial allocation of **150 to 400 BizCredits** (valued up to ₦3,500) credited to their wallet upon account verification at zero upfront cost, enabling immediate generation of:
   * A bank-ready technical business plan for capital acquisition.
   * 4K studio product shots of their physical prototypes and manufactured goods.
   * Corporate brand identity assets and commercial logo.
2. **Official "Verified Technical Innovator / NASENI Beneficiary" Badge:** A distinct verified trust badge embedded across their profile, storefront, and listings on `market.smartbizcoach.com.ng`.
3. **National B2B Wholesale Directory Priority:** Permanent inclusion on the B2B Wholesale trade pillar (`market.smartbizcoach.com.ng/?tab=B2B`), facilitating bulk supply contracts with nationwide distributors, commercial off-takers, and industrial projects.
4. **Tailored Engineering MOQ & Waybill Logistics:** Specialized configuration of Minimum Order Quantities (MOQ), technical spec sheets, and state-by-state waybill delivery schedules for heavy goods and machinery.
5. **Direct Pre-Underwriting for Federal Capital:** Prioritized generation of cryptographic SmartScore™ audit dossiers (`PublicAuditReport`) for submission to the Bank of Industry (BOI) Manufacturing Intervention Fund, SMEDAN matching grants, and partner commercial banks.
6. **CAC Legal Formalization Guidance:** Direct desk linkage with accredited Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) legal agents for formal incorporation as Limited Liability Companies (Ltd) at subsidized statutory rates.

## 7. FINANCIAL, TRANSACTIONAL & REVENUE FRAMEWORK

1. **Zero Financial Liability for NYE Secretariat:** This MOU imposes no mandatory subscription fee, financial liability, or administrative charge on the National Secretariat of NACCIMA Youth Entrepreneurs.
2. **Direct Paystack Settlement:** Commercial transactions conducted on the Market Square or storefronts are routed directly to the vendor's bank account via Paystack Subaccounts. SmartBiz Coach holds no vendor capital in custodial escrow.
3. **Project Implementation Agreements (PIAs):** Where dedicated donor funds, government intervention grants, or commercial sponsorships are secured under joint NYE–SmartBiz Coach initiatives, fund disbursement, administrative overheads, and project management fees shall be governed by mutually executed Project Implementation Agreements (PIAs).

## 8. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY, TECHNOLOGY & DATA GOVERNANCE

1. **Proprietary Technology Ownership:** SMARTBIZ COACH retains exclusive, undivided ownership, copyright, title, patent rights, source code, database architecture, and machine learning models powering `smartbizcoach.com.ng`.
2. **Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA 2023) Compliance:** Both Parties strictly adhere to the provisions of the NDPA 2023. All merchant financial transactions, debtor books, invoices, customer telephone records, and till entries remain the sole, confidential property of the respective business owner.
3. **Trademark & Co-Branding License:** Each Party grants the other a revocable, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use their official organizational name and logo exclusively for co-branded educational bootcamps, official certificates of completion, and joint grant submissions.

## 9. DURATION, RENEWAL, AND TERMINATION

1. **Initial Term:** This MOU shall take effect on the Effective Date (October 22, 2026) and shall remain valid for an initial term of **Two (2) Years**, subject to annual joint performance reviews.
2. **Automatic Renewal:** Upon expiration of the initial term, this MOU shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year periods unless either Party provides a thirty (30) day written notice of non-renewal.
3. **Termination for Convenience:** Either Party may terminate this agreement without cause upon giving **Thirty (30) Days** written notice to the other Party.
4. **Termination for Cause:** Either Party may terminate immediately upon written notice in the event of a material breach of terms, gross misconduct, or actions prejudicial to the other Party’s institutional integrity.

## 10. DISPUTE RESOLUTION, GOVERNING LAW & SIGNATURES

1. **Amicable Settlement:** Any dispute or divergence arising from the interpretation or implementation of this MOU shall be settled amicably through mutual executive consultations between the Founder & Chief Executive of SmartBiz Coach and the National Coordinator of NYE within **Fourteen (14) Days**.
2. **Governing Law:** This MOU shall be governed by, construed, and enforced in accordance with the Laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.
